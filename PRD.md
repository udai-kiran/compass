i# Compass

> **An AI-powered personal finance companion that helps people budget smarter, track financial goals, and make confident financial decisions.**

# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Vision

Compass is a modern personal finance application designed to help individuals and families understand their finances, stay within budget, and achieve long-term financial goals.

Rather than functioning as traditional accounting software, Compass focuses on financial awareness, intelligent automation, and actionable insights. The experience should feel like having a personal financial coach that works privately with your data.

---

# Goals

* Help users build sustainable financial habits.
* Simplify budgeting and expense tracking.
* Track progress toward financial goals.
* Provide a complete view of personal finances.
* Minimize manual effort through automation.
* Use AI to make financial management easier.
* Ensure users retain full ownership of their financial data.

---

# Target Users

* Individuals managing personal finances.
* Families tracking household spending.
* Professionals with multiple bank accounts and credit cards.
* Investors tracking long-term wealth.
* Anyone planning for future financial goals.
