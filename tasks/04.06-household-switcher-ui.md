**New nav entry — all three edits from `UI.md` required** (`NAV_GROUPS`, the closed `IconName` union, `CommandPalette` `PAGES`).

**Files:** `src/layouts/AppLayout.tsx` (switcher in the sidebar chrome), a new `src/routes/household/HouseholdPage.tsx`, `src/lib/household-queries.ts`, `src/components/icons.tsx`, `src/components/CommandPalette.tsx`.

- **Switcher** in `AppLayout`, showing the active household. **A solo user must see no added complexity** — with no household, the switcher is absent, not an empty dropdown.
- **Management page** — members and their roles, invite by token, accept, leave. Leaving is destructive: confirm, and state plainly what access is lost.
- **Person records** (from 4.1) are people, not logins — a spouse or child may exist with no account. The UI must make that obvious rather than implying everyone needs to sign up.
