**Codex P0.** The tax roadmap assumes salary, interest, dividends, rent, TDS and capital gains combine into one liability — but **only capital gains have a structured computation**. Interest, rent and dividends are ordinary categorized transactions with no payer, PAN/TAN, section, gross-versus-net split, TDS section, accrual period or AIS linkage.

`holdingEvents.type = "dividend"` exists, but `services/tax-lots.ts` deliberately ignores dividends because they are slab-taxed — and **nothing picks them up afterwards**. Bank interest and rental income have no structure at all.

Add an income-event model covering interest (194A/194K), dividends and IDCW, rent (194-I), and salary (192) — each with gross amount, TDS, payer, PAN/TAN, booking date and financial year, linked to a ledger transaction, holding, account or payslip.

**Tax facts need provenance and review status**, like `extracted_transactions`. A payslip, an AIS line, a manual entry and an inferred transaction can disagree; the model must record source priority, allow an override, prevent double-counting, and show why a figure was included. A tax total with no audit trail is not usable.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Income events with gross, TDS, payer, PAN/TAN, section, accrual period and FY
- [ ] Dividends from `holdingEvents` and interest from 11.3 flow in automatically
- [ ] Linked to source (transaction / holding / account / payslip) with provenance retained
- [ ] Source priority and user override defined; duplicates across sources prevented
- [ ] Review status per event — nothing silently enters a tax figure
- [ ] Audit trail explaining every included figure
- [ ] Tables added to `ALL_TABLES`/`USER_TABLES`
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
