Split out of the deduction basket precisely because **a nudge is only correct once the regime is known**. Under the new regime — the default since FY 2023-24 — almost no 80C/80D deduction applies, so telling such a user to "use your remaining ₹38k of 80C headroom" is actively wrong advice, not merely unhelpful.

Deadline-aware prompts, gated on the regime result from 11.8:
- 80C/80D headroom with days remaining to 31 March — **old regime only**
- PPF minimum contribution before dormancy (11.6)
- advance-tax instalment dates (11.10)
- ELSS lock-in and tax-saver FD maturity awareness (11.3, 12.3)

For a new-regime filer the honest output is "chasing 80C is pointless for you" — which is more useful than a tidy progress bar, and is the thing no other app will tell them.

Reuse the notification infrastructure and `alert_ledger` dedup rather than a parallel reminder system.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Nudges suppressed for new-regime users, **with the reason shown** rather than silently hidden
- [ ] Days-to-deadline surfaced; escalation as 31 March approaches
- [ ] Deduped via `alert_ledger`; honours notification preferences
- [ ] Never fires when the user has no actionable headroom
- [ ] Advance-tax reminders ahead of each statutory instalment date
