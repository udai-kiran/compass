Two features over the observation history:

**Buy now vs wait** — trend over observed prices for an item, with an explicit confidence signal. With three observations this is a guess and must say so; the failure mode to avoid is confident advice built on thin data.

**Price-honesty detector** — cross-reference a platform's "was ₹X, now ₹Y" claim against Compass's own observed history and flag inflated reference prices, which are endemic during festival sales.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Price history per item and source, charted with the existing `viz.tsx` primitives
- [ ] Buy-now-vs-wait states its confidence and refuses to advise below a minimum observation count
- [ ] Inflated "was" prices flagged against observed history, with the evidence shown
- [ ] Never fabricates a trend from a single observation
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
