> **Read [`UI.md`](./UI.md) first** — stack, the components that already exist, the three-edit nav rule, and the conventions this repo enforces. Do not add a UI or charting library.

2.4.0 adds thirteen backend tasks and, until this one, **no user-facing surface at all**.

**New nav entry** (all three edits) under **Plan**. **Files:** `src/routes/tax/TaxPage.tsx`, `src/lib/tax-queries.ts`, plus the three nav files.

- **Deduction basket** — 80C / 80CCD(1B) / 80CCD(2) / 80D as **separate** buckets with their own caps and `Meter` fills. Never pool them into one bar; the caps are legally distinct.
- **Regime comparison** — both regimes side by side with the crossover point stated. For a new-regime user, **say plainly that chasing 80C is pointless for them** rather than rendering a headroom bar that quietly does not apply.
- **Advance tax** — the four instalments with dates, amounts paid and 234B/234C exposure.
- **AIS/26AS reconciliation** — discrepancies as a reviewable list, using the staged-review pattern, never auto-applied.

Everything here is an **estimate**: label it, show the FY basis and the assumptions, and never present a figure as a filed position.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Four deduction buckets with distinct caps; never visually pooled
- [ ] Regime comparison with crossover; new-regime users told deductions do not apply to them
- [ ] Advance-tax schedule with due dates, paid amounts and interest exposure
- [ ] AIS/26AS discrepancies reviewable, never auto-applied
- [ ] Every figure labelled an estimate with its FY basis and assumptions visible
- [ ] Nav entry added via all three coordinated edits
- [ ] typecheck · lint · test · `npm run build -w apps/web` all pass
