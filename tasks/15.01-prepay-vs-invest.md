The most common real financial decision in an Indian household with a home loan, and one Compass is unusually equipped to answer because `emis.ts` already has `amortize`, `stepAmortization` and `splitInstallments`.

**Rate-reset impact** — repo-linked floating rates reset constantly, and lenders usually hold the EMI flat and extend the tenure instead, so the cost is invisible. Make it visible: *"8.5% → 9.0%: EMI unchanged, tenure +14 months, ₹4.2L extra interest."*

**Prepay vs invest** — compare interest saved by prepaying against the post-tax return of investing the same amount. Two subtleties decide the answer and are usually skipped:
- Interest saved is **risk-free and certain**; investment return is neither. Do not compare a guaranteed 9% against an assumed 12% as though they were equivalent.
- Under the old regime, section 24(b) relief on home-loan interest lowers the **effective** loan rate — so the answer depends on the user's regime (11.4), and flips for some people.

Also model tenure-reduction versus EMI-reduction prepayment, which have very different total-interest outcomes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Rate change → EMI/tenure/total-interest impact, using existing amortization
- [ ] Prepay vs invest compares interest saved against post-tax expected return, with risk asymmetry stated explicitly
- [ ] 24(b) effect applied only for old-regime users, sourced from 11.4
- [ ] Tenure-reduction vs EMI-reduction prepayment modelled separately
- [ ] Prepayment charges and lock-ins accounted for where recorded
- [ ] Willing to conclude "neither — build the emergency fund first" when that is correct
- [ ] Deterministic, integer paise, unit-tested against worked amortization examples
