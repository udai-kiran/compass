**Codex P2.** `services/anomaly.ts` already detects statistical amount outliers, but statistical surprise is not the same as **actionable wrongness**. A large legitimate purchase trips an anomaly; a ₹499 duplicate charge does not — and only one of them is money the household can get back.

Detect conclusions worth acting on, reusing merchant normalization, `transactionLinks`, card statement reconciliation, recurring-template history and the existing review inbox:
- duplicate charges at the same merchant within a short window
- repeated failed / reversed / charged sequences
- **subscription price increases** — the silent annual creep nobody notices
- unexpected ATM or cash withdrawal
- a refund that was promised and never arrived
- a charge appearing after a subscription was supposedly cancelled

Surface these into the existing review inbox rather than a new notification stream, so there is one place where "something needs your attention" lives.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Duplicate-charge detection using merchant normalization and a configurable window
- [ ] Subscription price increases detected against recurring-template history, with the delta shown
- [ ] Missing-refund detection via transaction links and elapsed time
- [ ] Post-cancellation charges flagged
- [ ] Findings land in the existing review inbox with dismissal that is remembered
- [ ] Tuned for precision over recall — a noisy fraud alert is worse than none
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
