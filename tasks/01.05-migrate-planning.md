Routes: budgets, goals, cashflow, bills, projection-settings, dashboard, insights, reports. Tables: budgets, budget_lines, budget_alerts, goals, subscription_dismissals, projection_settings.

`projection-settings` was already migrated by task 0.3 (it was the first slice of `modules/planning/`); 1.5 moved the remaining 7 route groups + 10 services in around it. `budget_lines` has no `user_id` and scopes via its parent budget. Goal projections are consumed later by the shopping goal-impact receipt (6.4).

Two clarifications to the original framing, established during 1.5 (`tasks/014-migrate-planning/`): (a) the budget-evaluation function `evaluateBudgetAlerts` physically lives in `services/notifications.ts` (a system-module file), not a planning file — planning owns the budget tables and `getUtilization`, but the alert dispatch stays in the shared alerts worker; (b) the only planning-table cron is `bills.remind`, plus the `autopilot.goals` cron which consumes the goal interface — budget-alert evaluation is reactive off `ledger.mutated`, not scheduled.
