Given a full list, solve for the cheapest way to split it across platforms — **cheapest total trip, not cheapest item-by-item**. Naive per-item minimums are actively misleading once delivery fees and minimum-cart thresholds are involved: three platforms at ₹40 delivery each usually beats nothing.

Deterministic optimization, not an LLM call — the model has no business doing arithmetic here. Inputs are observed prices, per-source delivery fees and minimum-cart thresholds; output is a split with a stated total and the saving versus single-source.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Optimizer accounts for delivery fees and minimum-cart thresholds, not just item prices
- [ ] Output states total cost and the delta versus the best single-source option
- [ ] Deterministic and unit-tested against hand-computed fixtures, including the case where splitting is *worse*
- [ ] Missing prices degrade gracefully — items without data are surfaced, not silently dropped
- [ ] All arithmetic in integer paise
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
