The payoff for the income ledger, and the piece of `PRD-wow-features.md` #2 that survives intact: **reconcile what the tax department already believes about you against what Compass knows.**

Import AIS/26AS (and Form 16) and match line by line against the 11.4 income events — interest the bank reported but you never recorded, dividends missing from your ledger, TDS credited under a PAN you forgot, a high-value transaction flagged in AIS.

Reuse the staged, reviewable import pattern from `services/imports.ts` and the matching approach from `statementReconciliations`: **staged, reviewable, reversible — never auto-applied.** Tax documents disagreeing with your ledger is normal and usually means *your ledger* is incomplete; the discrepancy is the product.

Given these documents contain PAN and full income detail, they are the most sensitive artifacts Compass will handle. Redaction before any model call and no raw logging are non-negotiable.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AIS/26AS and Form 16 imported through the staged, reversible import path
- [ ] Line-by-line matching against income events, with unmatched items on both sides surfaced
- [ ] Discrepancies presented as reviewable items, never auto-applied to the ledger
- [ ] TDS credits reconciled against recorded deductions
- [ ] PAN and income detail redacted before any model call; never logged raw
- [ ] Documents stored via the `Storage` abstraction
- [ ] Works with partial documents — a missing section does not fail the import
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
