`nominee` exists on exactly one table — `insurance_policies`. Bank accounts, holdings, EPF, PPF, NPS and FDs have no nominee field at all. Yet Compass holds the single most complete picture of a household's finances that will ever exist in one place.

The feature is a **continuity dossier**: every account, holding, policy and liability, with its nominee, its identifiers, and where the supporting document lives. The question it answers is the one nobody wants to think about — *"if something happens to me, can my family find and claim any of this?"* For most Indian households the honest answer is no, and the losses are real: unclaimed deposits and matured policies sit with institutions for decades.

Two India-specific points the feature must get right:

- **Nomination is not inheritance.** A nominee is a trustee who receives, not an owner who inherits — succession law or a will determines entitlement. Presenting nomination as inheritance would be actively misleading, and it is a widespread misconception.
- **Missing nominations are the common failure**, so the dossier's main job is surfacing accounts with no nominee recorded.

Exportable, because a dossier only reachable by logging into the deceased's self-hosted app helps nobody. Reuse the existing encrypted backup path rather than inventing a new export.

- [ ] Consolidated dossier view of every asset and liability with nominee, identifier and document link
