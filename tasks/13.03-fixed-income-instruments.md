**Codex's top P0 finding.** Compass claims Indian depth, yet a fixed deposit is merely `holdings.assetClass = "fd"` — no principal, interest rate, compounding convention, start date, maturity date, payout frequency, auto-renewal instruction, TDS, joint-holder status or premature-closure penalty. **RD and NSC are not modelled at all.**

This single gap blocks or weakens five planned tasks: the maturity calendar (12.3) cannot produce FD/RD/NSC maturities; the deduction basket (11.7) cannot identify a five-year tax-saver FD or NSC; advance tax (11.10) cannot accrue interest or TDS; rebalancing (5.7) cannot price a break penalty; and the windfall allocator (13.2) cannot recognise a maturing FD as a windfall.

Add typed deposit details and a **cash-flow schedule** — accrual and payout dates, not free-form notes — reusing `holdings`, `holdingValuations`, `holdingEvents`, goal linkage, the document pattern from `insurancePolicies` and the reminder infrastructure in `services/bills.ts`.

- [ ] Tax-saver (5-year) deposits flagged distinctly for 11.7
- [ ] Interest accrual schedule generated; bank TDS on interest recorded
- [ ] Maturity dates exposed for 12.3; break penalty exposed for 5.7
