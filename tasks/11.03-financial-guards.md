The three integrations that make this Compass's shopping feature rather than a generic one — each reusing financial machinery the app already has, consumed through the interfaces exposed in Phase 1.

**Budget cap** — a draft cart is checked against the live grocery envelope before it is ever shown (planning module, 1.5).

**Goal-impact receipt** — "this cart delays your Emergency Fund by 4 days", computed from existing goal and net-worth projections and shown *before* checkout, not after.

**EMI temptation guard** — a big-ticket item's "no-cost EMI" offer is run through `standardEmiPaise` and the amortization in the credit module, surfacing processing fees and opportunity cost. Most "no-cost" EMI is not free, and the app already knows how to prove it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Cart total checked against the live budget envelope; overage stated in rupees, not just flagged
- [ ] Goal-impact expressed in days of delay, from real projections, before checkout
- [ ] "No-cost" EMI offers decomposed into true cost via existing EMI math
- [ ] Guards inform and never block — the user can always proceed
- [ ] No goals or budgets configured degrades gracefully to no guard
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
