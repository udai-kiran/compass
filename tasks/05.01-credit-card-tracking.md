`card_details` extension on credit-card accounts: billing cycle day, due day, credit limit. Statement-period spend view, due-date & payment reminders, credit-utilization monitor with alert threshold.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Current statement period and amount due computed correctly across month boundaries
- [x] Reminders fire at configurable lead times before due date
- [x] Utilization % per card and overall, with alert over threshold

## Implementation
- `card_details` table (cycle day, due day, credit limit, utilization alert %, remind days, earn rate); `reward_entries` ledger. Migration 0006.
- `services/cards.ts`: `lastOccurrence`/`nextOccurrence` day-of-month math (statement close & due date across month/year boundaries), `listCards` (statement period, amount due at close, running balance, current-cycle spend, utilization %, reward balance), `evaluateCardDueReminders` (daily, dedup via `alert_ledger` `card-due:${accountId}:${dueDate}`), `evaluateCardUtilization` (per-period dedup `card-utilization:${accountId}:${period}`), rewards CRUD.
- `routes/cards.ts`: GET `/api/cards`, PUT `/api/cards/:id/details`, rewards list/add/delete. Registered in `app.ts`.
- Jobs: `cards.remind` daily scheduler + boot catch-up; `evaluateCardUtilization` in the alerts worker (fires via the ledger-write onResponse hook).
- Web: `routes/cards/CardsPage.tsx` (summary tiles, utilization `Meter`, cycle-setup form, rewards ledger) + `lib/card-queries.ts`; replaces the `/cards` stub.
- Verified end-to-end: statement 06-05→07-05 with ₹400 due, due reminder at configurable lead, utilization alert at 30% via the wired queue path.
