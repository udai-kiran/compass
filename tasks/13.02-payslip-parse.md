**Correction from review:** Compass does *not* parse payslips today. `services/epf-contributions.ts` accepts a **manually entered** amount and writes one income transaction carrying a `"payslip"` provenance tag (`PAYSLIP_TAG`), entered through `RecordEpfModal.tsx`. There is no parser and no payslip table.

This task builds the real thing: gross, basic, HRA, special allowance, employee and employer EPF, professional tax, and **TDS deducted to date** — and splits EPF into its employee / employer / EPS components rather than one combined positive transaction, which is insufficient for tax, pension or missing-contribution analysis.

Three downstream consumers make this the right first task in the phase: the 80C basket (11.2) needs employee EPF, the regime comparison (11.4) needs actual TDS and the HRA/allowance structure, and the income model (5.1) gets a far more reliable salary figure than ledger inference.

Reuse the extractor pattern verbatim — vision (3.1) or PDF text, redaction before the model, forced tool-calling gated on `ai.name !== "ollama"`, exact-name tool filtering, fail closed on 2+ matches, unparseable output never crashes. **Payslips are the most sensitive document the app will handle**, so `redactPii` and the existing "never log raw content" posture matter more here than anywhere else.

Parsed payslips are reviewable before they are trusted; a misread TDS figure propagates into tax advice.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Extracts gross, basic, HRA, allowances, employee/employer EPF, professional tax, TDS
- [ ] Handles the common Indian payslip layouts; unknown layouts degrade to manual entry
- [ ] Reviewable before commit, with the source document quoted as provenance
- [ ] Employee EPF flows to the 80C basket; employer EPF correctly treated as 80CCD(2)-style, not 80C
- [ ] TDS-to-date accumulates across months within a financial year
- [ ] PII redacted before any model call; raw payslip content never logged
- [ ] New `ai_events` kind; documents stored via the `Storage` abstraction
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
