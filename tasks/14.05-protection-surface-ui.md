> **Read [`UI.md`](./UI.md) first** — stack, the components that already exist, the three-edit nav rule, and the conventions this repo enforces. Do not add a UI or charting library.

**Files:** extend `src/routes/insurance/InsurancePage.tsx`; new `src/routes/insurance/AdequacyPanel.tsx`, `src/routes/protection/CalendarPage.tsx`, `src/routes/protection/DossierPage.tsx`. New nav entries under **Wealth** (all three edits).

- **Adequacy** — the term-life and health gap with its **workings shown**, not a score. Human Life Value has no single accepted formula, so the method, dependency duration, growth and inflation assumptions, and which assets were excluded from offset must all be visible. A bare number here is unjustifiable.
- **Usable versus headline cover** — a ₹10L floater with a 1% room-rent cap and 20% co-pay is not ₹10L. Show usable cover prominently; the sum assured is the less honest figure.
- **Calendar** — maturities and renewals on one dated view, including per-instalment ELSS unlock dates.
- **Dossier** — every asset and liability with nominee, identifier and document link. **Accounts with no nominee are the headline**, surfaced first. State clearly that nomination is not inheritance — a nominee receives as trustee; succession law decides entitlement.

Handle this material with care: it is about death and illness. Plain, factual, unsentimental — no urgency banners, no fear-based nudging.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Adequacy gap shown with full workings and stated assumptions, not a score
- [ ] Usable cover shown more prominently than headline sum assured
- [ ] Employer-owned cover flagged as a continuity risk
- [ ] Calendar covers maturities, renewals and per-instalment ELSS unlocks
- [ ] Dossier leads with missing nominations; states nomination ≠ inheritance
- [ ] Dossier exportable via the existing encrypted backup path
- [ ] Factual tone; no fear-based framing
