**Codex P0.** Compass knows scheme type, annual contribution transactions, maturity dates, holder and family dates of birth — and draws no compliance conclusion from any of it. These schemes have hard statutory limits where breaching or under-contributing has real consequences, and the deadline is silent.

FY-aware checks, using `fyOf()`/`fyRange()`, `accounts`, `retirementDetails`, `accountNpsDetails`, `sips` and the person model from 2.1:

- **PPF** — ₹500 minimum (a missed year makes the account dormant and needs a penalty to revive), ₹1.5L maximum, extension mode after 15 years
- **SSY** — beneficiary age eligibility, minimum/maximum annual contribution, the 15-year contribution window against a 21-year maturity, partial withdrawal at 18
- **NPS** — Tier I history, employer contribution, the separate 80CCD(1B) and 80CCD(2) buckets, age and exit constraints
- **Double-counting** — the same contribution claimed under two tax buckets
- **Year-end position** — excess or short contribution while there is still time to act

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Per-scheme FY contribution totals with minimum/maximum limits applied
- [ ] PPF dormancy risk flagged before year end, with the revival penalty stated
- [ ] SSY eligibility resolved against the actual child's date of birth via the person model
- [ ] NPS 80CCD(1B) and 80CCD(2) kept separate from the 80C basket
- [ ] Same contribution counted in two buckets detected and blocked
- [ ] Feeds both 11.7 (deduction basket) and 12.3 (maturity calendar)
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
