Closes the loop: photograph a real receipt, reconcile it against the drafted cart, and post the actual purchase to the ledger through the existing import/inbox review path. Confirmed purchases replenish the pantry (6.1) and sharpen consumption rates.

Two rules hold without exception. **Category stays manual** — the no-auto-categorization rule is not relaxed for shopping. And nothing reaches the ledger without an explicit accept, exactly as with email-extracted transactions.

Reconciliation reports what actually differed — bought but not on the list, on the list but not bought, price differences versus expectation.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Receipt photo → line items via the 3.1 vision path
- [ ] Reconciliation reports extra, missing, and price-differing items against the draft
- [ ] Accepted purchase becomes a ledger transaction with a **manually chosen** category
- [ ] Confirmed purchase replenishes pantry and updates consumption rates
- [ ] Unreadable receipt degrades to manual entry, never an error page
- [ ] Emits `ledger.mutated` so caches and budgets react (0.2)
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
