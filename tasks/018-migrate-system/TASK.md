## Objective
Move the "system" domain (routes health/auth/profile/notifications/backup + their 9 services and
colocated tests) into `apps/api/src/modules/system/`, following the 1.1–1.7 module template
(`plugin.ts`, thin re-export `schema.ts`, `routes/`, `services/`, `schema.smoke.test.ts`,
`plugin.test.ts`). **No runtime behaviour change**: URLs, handler bodies, SQL, table/column names all
invariant. The (method, URL) route SURFACE stays byte-identical; only the printRoutes TREE re-nests.
The distinguishing risk vs earlier migrations: this domain holds the app-level cross-cutting guards'
*consumers* — the auth `onRequest` guard, `config.public` public routes, demo-write 403 chokepoint,
CSRF Origin check, and the three rate-limit buckets must be PROVEN (by real encapsulation tests) to
still apply after the 5 routes move into an encapsulated plugin, not assumed from the route snapshot.
