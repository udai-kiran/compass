`habit_profiles` learns per-household consumption rate from repeat purchases in transaction history and from list submissions ("5kg atta lasts ~22 days here"). `pantry_items` tracks inferred stock, decremented over time by that rate and replenished on a confirmed purchase.

This is inference, not fact — the pantry is a model of reality and will drift. It must be trivially correctable by the user ("I still have plenty"), and correction must feed back into the rate rather than being overwritten on the next cycle.

Deterministic statistics, not an LLM call.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Consumption rate learned from purchase history; sparse history yields no confident rate
- [ ] Pantry level decays by rate and replenishes on confirmed purchase
- [ ] User correction is persistent and adjusts the learned rate
- [ ] Irregular or one-off purchases do not corrupt a rate
- [ ] Household-scoped via `withSharing()`
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
