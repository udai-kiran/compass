**Codex review finding: build this once, before 4.3.** Instrument guidance (4.3), the deduction basket (11.7), the maturity calendar (12.3) and rebalancing (5.7) all need the same facts about Indian instruments. Without a shared registry each will encode its own inconsistent copy, and they will disagree.

One versioned registry of instrument categories and their rules:

- **Lock-in** — ELSS 3 years *per instalment*, PPF 15 years with 5-year extension blocks, SSY to 21 years, NSC 5 years, tax-saver FD 5 years, SGB 8-year tenure with a year-5 exit window
- **Tax treatment** — deduction eligibility and section, gains treatment, exemption on maturity
- **Liquidity** — premature exit permitted, penalty, exit load
- **Horizon fit** — the suitability signal 4.3 ranks on

**Rules carry effective dates, not merely a financial year.** `tax-lots.ts` already demonstrates why: the 23 July 2024 holding-period reform and the section 50AA acquisition-date condition are mid-year changes whose applicability depends on acquisition or transfer date. A table keyed only by FY cannot represent Indian rules safely, and encoding one would produce confident wrong advice at exactly the boundaries that matter.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Registry of instrument categories with lock-in, tax, liquidity and horizon attributes
- [ ] **Rules keyed by effective date**, with separate acquisition / transaction / assessment applicability where they differ
- [ ] Per-instalment lock-in expressible (ELSS SIP), not just per-holding
- [ ] Consumed by 4.3; contract documented for 5.7, 11.7 and 12.3
- [ ] Data-driven — no inline constants in services
- [ ] A rule with no entry for a date fails loudly rather than defaulting silently
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
