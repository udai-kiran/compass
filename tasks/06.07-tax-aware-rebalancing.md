A rebalance that ignores tax and lock-ins can cost more than the drift it corrects. `services/capital-gains.ts` (`fyOf`, `fyRange`, `getCapitalGains`, FIFO matching) and `services/tax-lots.ts` give us lot matching to build on.

**Scope correction from review:** FIFO lot matching is a *primitive*, not a tax engine. True tax cost needs a regime and slab engine, other taxable income, surcharge/cess/rebate, loss set-off and carry-forward — none of which exists until 2.1.0. `tax-lots.ts` also documents that `gainsTaxClass` is **holding-wide**, so a mixed exchange-sale/RBI-redemption SGB position cannot be represented correctly.

**Therefore this task is deliberately constrained to realised gains and holding-period consequences.** It must not claim a rupee tax cost or an "optimal" decision. Full tax-cost rebalancing is a 2.1.0 follow-up once the rate engine exists. Lock-in constraints likewise depend on instrument modelling that mostly lands in 12.2 — see the sequencing note in `README.md`.

Extend the 5.4 plan with the real cost of each proposed switch:
- **Realised gains** — LTCG vs STCG per lot, using existing FIFO tax-lot matching
- **The LTCG allowance** — how much equity gain can be realised in this financial year before tax bites, and whether the switch fits inside it
- **Lock-ins and exit loads** — ELSS 3-year lock-in, PPF/EPF illiquidity, FD break penalties, SGB tenure
- **Timing** — whether waiting for a lot to cross the long-term threshold materially changes the cost

Show the tax cost next to the drift being corrected, so the user can see when the correction is not worth making. Sometimes the right answer is to leave it alone, and the app should be willing to say so.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Each proposed switch shows estimated realised gain, split long/short term, from real tax lots
- [ ] Current-FY LTCG headroom computed and applied
- [ ] Locked or penalised instruments excluded or flagged with their constraint
- [ ] Contribution-redirection correctly shown as the zero-tax route
- [ ] Explicitly recommends *not* rebalancing when tax cost exceeds the benefit
- [ ] Estimates labelled as estimates, with assumptions shown — never presented as filed tax positions
- [ ] Reuses existing capital-gains/tax-lot services rather than reimplementing FIFO
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
