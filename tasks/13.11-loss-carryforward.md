**Codex risk finding: ignoring losses can materially overstate tax and reverse a recommendation.** Advance tax, harvesting and rebalancing all need brought-forward and current-year losses; realised-gain totals alone are not a tax position.

Model Indian set-off ordering properly:
- short-term capital loss sets off against both short- and long-term gains
- long-term capital loss sets off **only** against long-term gains
- unabsorbed losses carry forward up to 8 assessment years, tracked per year of origin
- carry-forward requires a return filed within the due date — a condition worth surfacing rather than silently assuming

Consumed by advance tax (11.10), harvesting (11.12) and, later, tax-aware rebalancing.
