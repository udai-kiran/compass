**Codex P0.** One combined positive transaction is insufficient for tax, pension, employer matching or missing-contribution analysis — and that is all Compass records today.

It already stores the EPF account balance and UAN, `retirementDetails.epsBalancePaise`, manual contribution history, date of birth in `userProfiles`, and payroll-funded SIP intent (`sips.fundingSource = "payroll"`).

Add: employee, employer-EPF and **EPS components separately**; wage month and employer; passbook and payslip reconciliation; annual interest-credit reconciliation; **contribution gaps** (a month an employer did not deposit — a real and common problem); VPF rate; projected EPF and EPS benefit at retirement; and UAN continuity across job changes.

The contribution-gap check is the highest-value part: an employer quietly missing deposits is money the household loses permanently, and nothing else in their life will catch it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Employee / employer / EPS split per wage month, per employer
- [ ] Passbook or payslip reconciliation flags missing or mismatched months
- [ ] Interest credit reconciled annually against the declared rate
- [ ] VPF contributions distinguished from statutory EPF
- [ ] EPF and EPS benefit projected to retirement using date of birth and service history
- [ ] UAN continuity across employers; transfers not double-counted
- [ ] Employee contribution feeds 11.7 (80C); employer contribution correctly excluded from it
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
