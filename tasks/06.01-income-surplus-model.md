Everything in Phase 5 rests on one number Compass does not currently compute cleanly: **how much can this household actually invest each month?**

Derive reliable monthly income and investable surplus from the ledger and existing cashflow services — income minus committed outflows (EMIs, rent, insurance premiums, recurring bills, existing SIPs) — and be honest about variance rather than averaging it away. Indian salaried reality matters here: variable bonus, annual increments, EPF already deducted at source (already tracked via EPF contributions), and irregular freelance income.

A surplus figure that quietly assumes a bonus month is worse than no figure at all, so report a conservative baseline separately from an optimistic one.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Monthly income derived from the ledger, distinguishing recurring salary from irregular credits
- [ ] Committed outflows netted off: EMIs, recurring templates, bills, insurance premiums, active SIPs
- [ ] Conservative and optimistic surplus reported separately, with variance stated
- [ ] Sparse history yields low confidence rather than a fabricated number
- [ ] EPF already deducted at source is not double-counted as investable surplus
- [ ] Household-aware where a household exists (2.2), otherwise per-user
- [ ] Pure computation, unit-tested against fixtures
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
