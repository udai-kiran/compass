`maturityDate` currently lives in **three separate tables** — `retirement_details`, `insurance_policies` and `gold_details` — with nothing consolidating it, so money quietly matures into a savings account and sits there earning 3%.

**Correction from review: this is only partly a consolidation exercise.** FD, RD and NSC maturity dates do not exist anywhere — an FD is merely `holdings.assetClass = "fd"` with no principal, rate, compounding convention, payout frequency, auto-renewal instruction or premature-closure penalty, and RD and NSC are not modelled at all. Those instruments must be modelled before the calendar can cover them, which is the larger half of this task.

One dated calendar across everything that comes due:

- **FD / RD maturity** — and auto-renewal traps, where a matured FD silently rolls into a worse rate
- **PPF** — the 15-year maturity and its 5-year extension blocks (with and without further contribution)
- **SSY** — 21-year maturity, partial withdrawal at 18
- **SGB** — 8-year tenure with an exit window from year 5
- **ELSS lock-in** — **each SIP instalment carries its own 3-year lock-in**, so a monthly SIP produces a rolling stream of unlock dates, not one
- **NSC**, insurance renewals, EPF at retirement

The ELSS detail is the one most tools get wrong and the one that matters for rebalancing (5.4/5.5) — knowing which units are actually free to switch.

Reuse the bills/notification infra rather than building a parallel reminder system.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Single calendar consolidating maturity and renewal dates across all sources
- [ ] Per-instalment ELSS lock-in expiry computed correctly for SIPs
- [ ] PPF extension blocks and SSY partial-withdrawal milestone handled
- [ ] SGB exit window (year 5) distinguished from full tenure (year 8)
- [ ] Reminders ahead of each date, deduped via `alert_ledger`, honouring notification prefs
- [ ] Feeds 5.4/5.5 so rebalancing knows which units are unlocked
- [ ] Surfaces "matured and idle" money already sitting in a low-yield account
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
