Tables `splits`, `split_shares`, `settlements`. On any transaction, choose members and a split rule (equal / shares / exact); Compass maintains a running balance ledger ("Priya owes you ₹1,240") and a settle-up action that records the settling transfer.

All amounts are integer paise. **Split shares must always sum exactly to the transaction amount, with the household balance summing to zero across members** — this is an invariant, not a validation, and equal splits with a remainder (₹100 across 3) must distribute the leftover paise deterministically rather than losing or inventing money.

Scope is deliberately narrow: equal/shares/exact only, no multi-currency, no groups-of-groups. Settle-up records a transfer; it never moves money.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Split shares sum exactly to the transaction amount in integer paise, for all three rules
- [ ] Remainder distribution on indivisible equal splits is deterministic and tested
- [ ] Household balances sum to zero across members (invariant test)
- [ ] Settle-up records a transfer and zeroes the pair's balance; no money movement rails
- [ ] Deleting or editing a split transaction leaves balances consistent
- [ ] Tables added to `ALL_TABLES`/`USER_TABLES`
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
