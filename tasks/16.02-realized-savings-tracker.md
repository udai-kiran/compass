**The feature a standalone comparator structurally cannot build**, and the clearest expression of why shopping belongs inside Compass rather than beside it.

A comparison app tells you Blinkit is ₹30 cheaper and then forgets. It has no idea what you actually bought, where, or for how much. Compass reconciles the real receipt into the ledger (8.4), so it can close the loop and answer the question that actually changes behaviour: **what did this household save, and what did it leave on the table?**

Per purchase, and cumulatively:
- **Realized saving** — what you paid versus the best available option at the time, including the card/offer effect from 7.4
- **Money left on the table** — the saving that was available and not taken, which is the more motivating number and the one nobody else can compute
- **Where it went** — by platform, category and card, so the pattern is actionable rather than a single guilt-inducing total
- **Whether the advice was any good** — did following a recommendation actually save what it predicted

That last point matters most and should not be quietly dropped: a savings tracker that only measures the user, and never itself, is marketing. If the optimizer's predictions do not survive contact with real receipts, the honest response is to show that and fix the optimizer.

Comparisons use the price observations that existed **at the time of purchase**, never today's prices — scoring past decisions against later data would be meaningless.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Per-purchase realized saving computed against contemporaneous observations, in integer paise
- [ ] "Left on the table" reported alongside savings, broken down by platform, category and card
- [ ] Predicted versus actual saving tracked, so recommendation quality is measurable
- [ ] Purchases with no contemporaneous price data excluded rather than assumed break-even
- [ ] Cumulative view over any period, reusing existing report primitives
- [ ] Framed as information, never as scolding for a "missed" saving
- [ ] typecheck + lint + test green
