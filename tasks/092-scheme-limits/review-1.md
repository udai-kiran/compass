## High

- **H1 — The plan cannot evaluate account-opening eligibility or lifecycle from the existing schema.** `P1` requires `accountOpenedDate`, but `accounts` stores only the Compass row’s `createdAt`, not the scheme opening date ([hubs.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/db/shared/hubs.ts:66)). `retirement_details` stores only a manually entered `maturityDate` and has no PPF extension mode/block ([schema.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/protection/schema.ts:33)); NPS details likewise have no joining date ([schema.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/investments/schema.ts:41)). Therefore SSY’s opening-age and 15-year deposit-window checks, PPF maturity/extension, and NPS tenure-based exit checks cannot be implemented reliably while AC6 prohibits new persisted data. Persist the authoritative scheme opening date and PPF extension state. PPF maturity is also not simply opening date plus 15 years: it occurs after 15 years from the end of the opening FY. [Official PPF rules](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/schemesandservices/posb/PublicProvidentFundScheme2019English.pdf).

- **H2 — NPS 80CCD(1), 80CCD(1B), and 80CCD(2) cannot be calculated from the proposed inputs.** The pure function receives only FY, DOB, opening date, and total contribution ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:79)). It lacks regime, employment status, salary, gross total income, employer type, and separate employee/employer contributions. Section 80CCD(1) is the lower of the contribution and 10% of statutory salary for an employee, but 20% of gross total income for a non-employee; “salary” includes qualifying DA and turnover-based commission. The plan omits the non-employee path entirely. [Income Tax Department deduction rules](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/deductions), [section 80CCD](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-80ccd-21). The existing payslip vocabulary contains `basic` but no DA component, employer type, or specific employee/employer NPS kinds ([tax.ts](/work/personal/compass/packages/shared/src/schemas/tax.ts:76)). Consequently the employer-rate matrix in [tax-rules.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/lib/tax-rules.ts:460) cannot actually be applied.

- **H3 — The double-counting design cannot detect double counting.** There is no persisted tax claim/allocation model, and ledger transactions do not say which contribution amount was assigned to 80CCD(1) versus 80CCD(1B). Comparing equal amounts would create false positives because two legitimate contributions can have the same value; aggregating totals loses contribution identity. The proposed result has exactly one `deductionBucket`, so it cannot represent one NPS contribution pool split across 80CCD(1), 80CCD(1B), and employer 80CCD(2) ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:53)). Section 80CCD(1B) prohibits deduction only for the amount already claimed under 80CCD(1), which requires allocation/provenance, not amount equality. [Section 80CCD(1B)](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-80ccd-21). Introduce contribution-level identifiers and allocations, or define a deterministic allocator that emits mutually exclusive allocated amounts; do not call this “detection” without claim data.

- **H4 — Existing transactions cannot reliably supply “annual contributions.”** `transactions` has no account or amount column; amounts and accounts live on `postings` ([ledger.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/db/shared/ledger.ts:22), [ledger.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/db/shared/ledger.ts:107)). Even summing positive scheme-account postings would mix deposits with opening balances, interest credits, adjustments, and possibly other inflows; netting postings would also let withdrawals reduce gross annual deposits. Nothing currently classifies a posting as employee NPS, employer NPS, PPF/SSY deposit, interest, or refund. Thus P3 cannot enforce deposit maxima or derive tax buckets from “transactions aggregated by FY” without typed contribution provenance.

- **H5 — SSY DOB is reachable, but full eligibility is not representable.** Family-member DOBs are held in `family_members.date_of_birth` ([persons.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/db/shared/persons.ts:35)), referenced by `accounts.holder_id` ([hubs.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/db/shared/hubs.ts:85)). `user_profiles.date_of_birth` is only the current user’s DOB, not a child beneficiary’s ([system schema](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/system/schema.ts:32)). However, `family_members` has no sex/gender field, so the service cannot establish that the beneficiary is a girl; relationship=`child` is insufficient. The query must also require a holder, join with both `family_members.id = accounts.holder_id` and matching `userId`, and distinguish missing DOB from ineligibility. The official rule requires a girl child under ten on the actual opening date. [Official SSY rules](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/schemesandservices/posb/SukanyaSamriddhiAccountScheme2019English.pdf).

- **H6 — “Exit age: 60” is obsolete and the proposed API has no data with which to test an exit.** Current PFRDA rules distinguish normal/premature exit by model, tenure, age/retirement, corpus, and requested exit; the All Citizen model now describes normal exit after age 60 or 15 years and an entry/exit ceiling of 85. The plan supplies none of sector/model, joining date, corpus, retirement date, or an exit request, while the result schema has no exit-specific finding. [Current PFRDA All Citizen rules](https://www.pfrda.org.in/en/schemes/national-pension-system/nps-for-all-citizen-models), [July 2026 regulations](https://pfrda.org.in/w/pension-fund-regulatory-and-development-authority-exits-and-withdrawals-under-the-national-pension-system-regulations-2015-last-amended-on-20th-july-2026-.?p_l_back_url=%2Fhi%2Fweb%2Fpfrda%2Frecent-updates%3Fdelta%3D60%26start%3D1&p_l_back_url_title=Recent+Updates). Exit compliance should be effective-dated and separately modeled, or removed from this task.

## Medium

- **M1 — PPF extension mode is named but neither modeled nor specified.** After maturity, an account may continue without deposits, or opt within one year for contribution-enabled five-year blocks. Deposits without a timely extension election are irregular and refundable without interest; after continuing without deposits for more than a year, contribution mode cannot be resumed. Extension-with-deposits also has a 60% block-period withdrawal ceiling. A boolean “extended” is insufficient: persist mode, election date, and block dates. [Official PPF rules](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/schemesandservices/posb/PublicProvidentFundScheme2019English.pdf).

- **M2 — Several PPF compliance semantics are incorrect or incomplete.** Below ₹500 after the initial year makes an account “discontinued,” not dormant; during the open FY it is merely at risk until March 31. Revival requires a ₹50 fee **plus ₹500 arrears for every default year**, not a “₹500 penalty” as stated in the root-cause text ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:9)). Deposits must be in multiples of ₹50, and the ₹1.5 lakh ceiling is aggregated across an individual’s own PPF and minor accounts, so per-account checks alone can miss a breach. [Official PPF rules](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/schemesandservices/posb/PublicProvidentFundScheme2019English.pdf).

- **M3 — SSY rules are under-specified.** Missing checks include one account per girl, normally no more than two girl beneficiaries per family with multiple-birth exceptions, deposits in multiples of ₹50, and the ₹50-per-default-year regularization rule. The partial-withdrawal statement is also inaccurate: education withdrawal is allowed after age 18 **or passing class 10, whichever is earlier**, is capped against the preceding FY balance, and cannot exceed the documented education requirement. [Official SSY rules](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/schemesandservices/posb/SukanyaSamriddhiAccountScheme2019English.pdf).

- **M4 — `deductiblePaise` overclaims what this service can know.** PPF and SSY do not each receive an independent ₹1.5 lakh deduction: they share the aggregate 80C/80CCC/80CCD(1) ceiling, and those deductions are unavailable under the new regime. Likewise, 80CCD(1) belongs to the section 80CCE aggregate; calling it an “80C deduction bucket” is imprecise. Because the result has no regime or other basket contributions, it can expose an `eligibleContributionPaise`, but not “what can actually be claimed.” The aggregate limit is already correctly described in [tax-rules.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/lib/tax-rules.ts:442).

- **M5 — The result type cannot represent important states.** NPS has no fixed maximum, but `maxPaise` and `headroomPaise` are mandatory numbers, making infinity/headroom undefined. A single `statusCode` also loses simultaneous findings such as an ineligible SSY account that is above its deposit maximum. Use nullable fixed caps and an array of structured findings; represent tax allocations separately from scheme-operational compliance.

- **M6 — FY support is not bounded consistently.** `FySchema` and `financial-year.ts` accept any canonical FY ([financial-year.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/lib/financial-year.ts:41), [tax.ts](/work/personal/compass/packages/shared/src/schemas/tax.ts:12)), while the NPS employer matrix covers only FY 2023-24 through 2026-27. The route must define a clear unsupported-FY response instead of accepting a valid FY and failing during lookup.

## Low

- **L1 — The proposed tests are too narrow for the stated guarantees.** Add cases for missing holder/DOB/opening date, holder ownership, exact tenth birthday, PPF end-of-opening-FY maturity, SSY 15-year anniversary boundaries, current versus completed FY default status, multiple PPF/SSY accounts, contribution-versus-interest classification, salaried versus self-employed 80CCD(1), old/new regime, and employee/employer contribution separation. Tests should also cover PPF/SSY aggregate caps and prove that equal-valued independent NPS contributions are not treated as duplicates.
