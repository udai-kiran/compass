The revised plan still needs changes before implementation.

## High

- **H1 — The result contract cannot represent the lifecycle and eligibility states the revised plan requires.** The plan says a missing `scheme_opened_date` produces `data_missing`, and SSY age failure produces `data_invalid` ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:13), [TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:54)), but `statusCode` permits neither value and has no final `discontinued` state—only `discontinued_risk` ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:67)). Add structured lifecycle/eligibility findings, or at least `data_missing`, `data_invalid`, `discontinued`, and `outside_deposit_window`. Current-FY shortfall is a risk; a completed deficient FY is an actual default/discontinuation.

- **H2 — `scheme_opened_date` is sufficient for SSY’s initial 15-year deposit window, SSY opening-age calculation, and PPF’s original maturity date, but not for post-maturity PPF compliance.** SSY permits deposits until 15 years from the actual opening date and matures after 21 years, while PPF maturity is after 15 years from the end of the opening FY—not simply `openedDate + 15 years`. [Official SSY Scheme](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/schemesandservices/posb/SukanyaSamriddhiAccountScheme2019English.pdf), [official PPF Scheme](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/schemesandservices/posb/PublicProvidentFundScheme2019English.pdf). Because PPF may continue without deposits or in elected five-year contribution blocks, an opening date alone cannot decide whether post-maturity deposits are permitted or whether a minimum applies. Since extension mode is explicitly out of scope ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:126)), the service must return an unknown/not-evaluated lifecycle finding after original maturity rather than applying normal limits.

- **H3 — The proposed postings query does not safely calculate annual contributions.** The FY date predicate is conceptually correct and inclusive, because `transactions.date` is an authoritative PostgreSQL `DATE` ([ledger.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/db/shared/ledger.ts:29)) and `fyRange()` returns April 1 through March 31 inclusive ([financial-year.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/lib/financial-year.ts:75)). However, the shown query:
  
  - omits `transactions.userId = userId`, violating the repository’s mandatory user-scoping rule;
  - omits `transactions.deletedAt IS NULL`, so soft-deleted contributions remain counted;
  - includes positive opening-balance postings, which Compass creates as real transactions;
  - uses `fyRange.start/end`, although `fyRange()` returns a tuple, not an object;
  - includes transfers, corrections, refunds, imported interest credits, and any other positive posting.

  Existing aggregation code explicitly filters both owner and soft deletion ([accounts.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/ledger/services/accounts.ts:198)). The claim that Compass contains only user-initiated inflows is contradicted by transaction sources including `import` and `recurring` ([ledger.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/db/shared/ledger.ts:20)). `isEstimate` does not make opening balances or deleted rows an acceptable contribution estimate. At minimum, exclude deleted rows and opening transactions, enforce user ownership, and document that unclassified positive non-opening inflows remain an approximation.

- **H4 — The NPS outputs still double-count the same contribution.** Setting `eligibleNpsCcd1Paise = contributed` and `eligibleNpsCcd1bPaise = min(contributed, cap)` presents overlapping amounts as simultaneously eligible ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:71)). Section 80CCD(1B) applies only to the portion not claimed under 80CCD(1); after allocating the entire contribution to CCD(1), nothing remains for CCD(1B). [Income Tax Department guidance](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/individual-business-profession). If allocation belongs to 13.7, 13.6 should expose a single `npsEmployeeContributionPaise` plus applicable caps—not two “eligible” amounts. Alternatively, define a mutually exclusive allocation, such as:
  
  `ccd1b = min(contribution, 5_000_000)` and `ccd1 = contribution - ccd1b`,
  
  while making clear that 13.7/13.8 may choose a different allocation.

- **H5 — Raw NPS contribution is acceptable, but calling it `eligibleNpsCcd1Paise` is not.** The actual CCD(1) eligible amount depends on 10% of statutory salary for employees or 20% of gross total income for non-employees, as well as the aggregate ₹1.5 lakh limit. Deferring salary-dependent calculation is reasonable, but the field must be named something like `npsEmployeeContributionPaise` or `potentialCcd1ContributionPaise`. The planned note that 13.8 will apply “the salary cap” also omits the non-employee 20%-of-GTI path. The existing tax rules correctly describe CCD(1) as part of the aggregate limit ([tax-rules.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/lib/tax-rules.ts:442)).

- **H6 — Several displayed formulas use rupees as though they were paise.** In a `...Paise` field, ₹1.5 lakh is `15_000_000`, not `150000`, and ₹50,000 is `5_000_000`, not `50000` ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:70)). Implementing the pseudocode literally would cap 80C at ₹1,500 and CCD(1B) at ₹500. The repository’s existing tax data uses the correct conversion through `lakh(1.5)` and `lakh(0.5)` ([tax-rules.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/lib/tax-rules.ts:446), [tax-rules.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/lib/tax-rules.ts:455)). The PPF revival wording also confuses `5000` paise with “₹5,000”; the revival amount is the ₹50 fee plus ₹500 arrears for each default year.

- **H7 — The service plan does not ensure that only NPS Tier I accounts are returned.** `accounts.type = "nps"` does not distinguish Tier I from Tier II; tier is stored in `account_nps_details.tier` ([schema.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/investments/schema.ts:41)). P4 says it loads accounts “by scheme type” ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:104)), which would incorrectly report Tier II as `nps_tier1` and attach tax benefits. The query must join `accountNpsDetails`, scope that row by `userId`, and require `tier = 'tier_i'`. Define how an NPS account with missing detail data is reported.

## Medium

- **M1 — The plan modifies the wrong shared account schema file and does not provide a write path for the opening date.** Account contracts live in [ledger.ts](/work/personal/compass/packages/shared/src/schemas/ledger.ts:183), not `schemas/wealth.ts`. `AccountSchema`, `CreateAccountSchema`, and `UpdateAccountSchema` currently lack the field ([ledger.ts](/work/personal/compass/packages/shared/src/schemas/ledger.ts:183), [ledger.ts](/work/personal/compass/packages/shared/src/schemas/ledger.ts:243)), and `toAccount()` must also return it ([accounts.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/ledger/services/accounts.ts:136)). Adding only the database column and a response schema leaves users unable to populate or correct it through the account API.

- **M2 — Per-account eligible contribution is acceptable as allocator input, but not as true double-counting detection or complete PPF-limit compliance.** Deferring claim allocation to 13.7 is a valid narrowing of H3, provided 13.6 does not claim to detect duplicates. However, the PPF ₹1.5 lakh statutory deposit maximum is inclusive of an individual’s own account and minor accounts, not always independent per account. [Official PPF Scheme](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/schemesandservices/posb/PublicProvidentFundScheme2019English.pdf). Therefore per-account `headroomPaise` can miss an aggregate statutory breach. Either state that cross-account PPF maximum enforcement is deferred, or aggregate accounts by subscriber/guardian.

- **M3 — DOB-only SSY checking is acceptable as a partial check, but ownership and missing-data behavior must be explicit.** `holderId` reaches `family_members.date_of_birth` ([hubs.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/db/shared/hubs.ts:91), [persons.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/db/shared/persons.ts:44)), so age on the actual opening date can be calculated. The join must also require `family_members.userId = userId`; the foreign keys do not enforce that account and holder belong to the same user. Missing holder, DOB, or opening date must produce an unknown/data-missing finding rather than `ok`. Noting that gender was not verified is acceptable, but the result must not describe overall SSY eligibility as verified.

- **M4 — Deposit-multiple rules are declared but neither returned nor tested.** `minDepositMultiple` is part of `SchemeRules` ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:45)), but the result has no invalid-multiple finding and P8 includes no multiple-boundary tests. PPF and SSY deposits must be in ₹50 multiples. An annual aggregate alone also cannot prove that each individual deposit met the rule; this requires examining contribution postings individually.

- **M5 — `eligible80CPaise` is still potentially misleading.** Per-account pre-allocation contribution is useful, but 80C is old-regime-only and shares the ₹1.5 lakh aggregate with 80CCC and CCD(1), as already encoded in [tax-rules.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/lib/tax-rules.ts:442). Prefer `potential80CContributionPaise` or a generic `eligibleContributionPaise` tagged with its candidate section. Define non-applicable fields as zero or nullable; otherwise every PPF/SSY result unnecessarily carries NPS fields and vice versa.

- **M6 — NPS employer contribution is mentioned without implementable inputs.** The plan says CCD(2) is computed separately from the employer-rate matrix ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/092-scheme-limits/TASK.md:56)), but the result has no CCD(2) field and the postings cannot distinguish employee from employer contributions. Remove this statement from 13.6 or explicitly identify the later task and required provenance.

- **M7 — Descope of NPS exit compliance is acceptable.** Exit compliance requires substantially different sector, joining, corpus, retirement, and requested-exit data. Removing it entirely from this task is cleaner than emitting an unreliable age-only result.

## Low

- **L1 — The planned tests do not cover the revised design’s important boundaries.** Add tests for soft-deleted and opening transactions, cross-user transaction/holder isolation, imported positive credits, exact FY endpoints, missing opening date/holder/DOB/NPS details, Tier II exclusion, SSY’s exact tenth birthday, exact 15-year SSY boundary, PPF maturity based on end of opening FY, current versus completed FY shortfall, paise cap values, and non-overlap of CCD(1)/CCD(1B).

- **L2 — The rules are described as FY-aware but are effectively constant.** That is acceptable if the implementation still rejects unsupported FYs consistently. `FySchema`/`fyRange()` accept any canonical FY, while the current tax-rule table is bounded to FY 2023-24 through FY 2026-27. Define whether scheme rules apply indefinitely or are effective-dated and fail closed outside known periods.
