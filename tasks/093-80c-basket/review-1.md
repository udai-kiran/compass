## High

1. **The NPS allocation is internally contradictory and incomplete.** The task says both “first ₹1.5L … to 80CCD(1)” and “80CCD(1B) first” ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/093-80c-basket/TASK.md:48)). Tax law does not prescribe a chronological “first” attribution: 80CCD(1B) permits up to ₹50,000 that is not also claimed under 80CCD(1). Allocating up to ₹50,000 to 1B first is a reasonable tax-optimizing policy, but it must be documented as an allocation policy, not a legal rule. The remaining 80CCD(1) amount must also respect the employee 10%-of-salary/non-employee 20%-of-GTI limit and the shared 80CCE ₹1.5 lakh ceiling. Automatic and manual NPS amounts also need one mutually exclusive allocation pool to prevent double counting. [Income Tax Department deduction guidance](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/deductions).

2. **The planned 80CCD(2) treatment is legally wrong.** It is not simply “no cap for old regime” ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/093-80c-basket/TASK.md:54)); it is capped as a percentage of statutory salary, and it remains available under the new regime. The repository already models the relevant private/government and old/new-regime rates correctly in [tax-rules.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/lib/tax-rules.ts:460). A manual Form 16 amount can be the source, but `claimedPaise` must apply the appropriate FY/regime/employer rate, or explicitly remain unvalidated. [Official threshold guidance](https://wmstatic-prd.incometaxindia.gov.in/web/guest/w/threshold-limits-under-income-tax-act).

3. **Insurance deductions should not be sourced from annualized policy terms.** `premiumPaise` is explicitly the amount per scheduled payment, while `premiumFrequency` describes contractual cadence ([spines.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/db/shared/spines.ts:143)). Those fields can derive an expected annual amount—monthly ×12, quarterly ×4, half-yearly ×2, yearly ×1—but not the amount paid in the requested FY. The app already records actual, dated premium transactions via `transactions.policyId` and exposes their payment history ([insurance.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/protection/services/insurance.ts:300)). Both 80C and 80D are payment-based deductions; use FY-filtered, non-deleted premium transactions as the primary source and annualized terms only as an explicitly labelled estimate. “Single” frequency especially cannot be annualized across later FYs. [Section 80D](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-80d-62).

4. **The home-loan calculation would materially overstate deductible amounts.** Section 24(b) is not universally “full deduction under old regime” ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/093-80c-basket/TASK.md:62)): self-occupied property normally has an aggregate ₹2 lakh or ₹30,000 ceiling depending on purpose/date/completion conditions; let-out-property treatment differs, and pre-construction interest is handled separately. The current account/EMI schema does not store property occupancy, loan purpose, borrowing/completion dates, ownership, or lender certificate amounts. Moreover, only `home_loan_od` is distinguishable by account type; a conventional home loan can be an undifferentiated `loan`. The basket should expose an estimated EMI interest split, not label it deductible 24(b), until these facts are available. [Official section 24 guidance](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-24-64).

5. **Tasks 13.5 and 13.6 are not implemented dependencies in the current tree.** Their task files are `APPROVED`, not complete, and there are no `epf_contributions`, scheme-compliance service, or `schemeOpenedDate` implementations under `apps/api/src`. Consequently the planned automatic EPF, PPF, SSY, and NPS sources do not currently exist, despite being treated as available dependencies ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/093-80c-basket/TASK.md:88)). Task 13.7 must either wait for those implementations or include explicit fallback queries and revise its dependency/verification plan.

## Medium

1. **`isTaxSaverFd` would duplicate an existing discriminator and is proposed in the wrong module.** `deposit_details` lives in [apps/api/src/modules/investments/schema.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/investments/schema.ts:182), not `modules/tax/schema.ts`. Its existing `depositKind` enum already contains `tax_saver_fd`, and the service validates its five-year term ([deposit-details.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/investments/services/deposit-details.ts:104)). Use `depositKind = 'tax_saver_fd'`; adding a boolean would permit contradictory states such as `depositKind='rd'` plus `isTaxSaverFd=true`.

2. **`isElss` is a reasonable new field, but the plan does not make it usable or enforce its domain.** No existing field identifies ELSS: `assetClass='mutual_fund'` and `gainsTaxClass='equity'` only describe instrument and capital-gains treatment, so they cannot distinguish ELSS from ordinary equity funds ([spines.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/db/shared/spines.ts:38)). A boolean is adequate if ELSS is the only needed tax-saving MF subtype, but it should be exposed through `HoldingSchema`, create/update contracts, response mapping, UI/import paths, and validated to apply only to mutual-fund holdings. P1 currently mentions only the DB column and migration.

3. **The 80D model cannot correctly allocate actual health premiums.** The proposed fixed ₹25,000 self/family bucket misses the ₹50,000 self/family limit when the taxpayer or spouse is senior; the existing tax rules already contain this variant ([tax-rules.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/lib/tax-rules.ts:500)). Policies must also be allocated according to covered people, not merely whether any parent exists. `policy_covered_persons` provides structured person links, whereas free-form `coveredMembers` does not. Preventive check-ups have a single aggregate ₹5,000 sub-limit inside the two 80D group limits, not an additional standalone allowance. The plan also omits eligible uninsured-senior medical expenditure. [Section 80D rules](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-80d-62).

4. **EMI splitting exists, but it must run from loan inception before FY filtering.** `splitInstallments` already produces principal/interest per actual payment, and `listEmiInstallments` safely obtains owned, non-deleted, outflow-signed transactions ([emis.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/credit/services/emis.ts:456)). The basket can reuse that service. It must not pass only the requested FY’s payments into `splitInstallments`, because the opening principal for that FY depends on every earlier installment. Compute the full history from `startDate`, then select rows in the FY. Even then, it remains an estimate compared with a lender’s annual interest certificate.

5. **Automatic NSC data already exists and is being ignored.** `depositKind` includes `nsc`, with principal and start date in `deposit_details` ([schema.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/investments/schema.ts:182)). Treating NSC solely as a manual entry contradicts the objective of aggregating existing data. At minimum, initial principal should be sourced automatically in its start FY. If the scope claims comprehensive NSC deductions, it must also address eligible accrued-and-reinvested interest in applicable years.

6. **EPF source precedence is underspecified and risks double counting.** “From reconciliation OR payslip components” needs deterministic precedence. Task 13.5 defines actual confirmed amounts as canonical, expected reconciliation amounts as fallback, and payslip components as the origin of those expected values. The basket should consume the reconciled FY result once, not independently sum both sources, and should preserve estimated/confirmed provenance per item.

7. **The manual-entry schema permits invalid and duplicate classifications.** `section` and `deduction_kind` are unrestricted text, with no database check or shared enum ensuring compatible pairs. It allows entries such as `section='80D', deduction_kind='tuition_fees'`, and manual ELSS/NPS entries can duplicate automatic sources. Add constrained enums/checks, an explicit source/provenance model, FY validation, and a policy for whether manual entries supplement or override automatic data. `source_doc_key` also needs to follow an existing storage/attachment lifecycle rather than being an orphaned string.

8. **The regime contract does not match existing code.** The task references `getUserRegimePreference` and an `unknown` regime ([TASK.md](/work/personal/compass/tasks/093-80c-basket/TASK.md:67)), but the existing function is `getRegimePreference`, and it always resolves to `old` or `new`, defaulting to `new` ([regime-preference.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/tax/services/regime-preference.ts:76)). Either use that contract or explicitly redefine “unknown” to mean no chosen/inferred preference. Also clarify that new-regime suppression applies to 80C/80CCD(1B)/80D headroom, not to 80CCD(2).

9. **Caps should come from FY-versioned tax rules rather than task literals.** `getDeductionCap()` and FY-specific 80C, 80CCD(1B), 80CCD(2), and 80D variants already exist in [tax-rules.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/lib/tax-rules.ts:428). Hard-coding caps in the basket would create a second legal-rule source and bypass the existing fail-loudly behavior for unsupported FYs.

10. **“All deduction sources” overstates the defined scope.** The table cannot represent 80CCC separately even though it shares the 80CCE ceiling with 80C and 80CCD(1). Other common 80C sources—eligible pension/annuity products, stamp duty/registration charges, SCSS, and post-office deposits—have no explicit source kinds. Either add appropriate manual kinds/sections or narrow the objective to the listed sources.

## Low

1. **`alert_ledger` is sufficient for deduplication as designed.** It has the correct unique key on `(userId, kind, refKey)` ([system/schema.ts](/work/personal/compass/apps/api/src/modules/system/schema.ts:60)), and existing jobs atomically insert with `onConflictDoNothing()`. No schema change is needed. Correctness depends on tax jobs using stable occurrence-specific keys—for example `{fy}:{tier}` for escalating 80C alerts and `{accountId}:{fy}` for PPF—rather than a bare FY that would suppress distinct alert tiers.

2. **The basket’s `totalPaise`, `claimedPaise`, and `headroomPaise` semantics need explicit definitions.** The plan does not say whether `totalPaise` is raw paid, legally eligible before the bucket cap, or allocated after cross-section limits; nor whether `claimedPaise` becomes zero under the new regime. Define these stages in the pure-function input/output and add reconciliation invariants so one contribution cannot appear in multiple claimed buckets.
